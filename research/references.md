# References and Source Verification

This file will contain only references whose bibliographic metadata and relevance have been checked.

## Verification rules

For every source:
1. Verify title, authors, publication venue, year, and DOI against the publisher or a trusted scholarly index.
2. Confirm whether it is peer-reviewed, a preprint, a thesis, or technical documentation.
3. Read the full text where the method, split protocol, or limitation is important.
4. Record the exact claim supported by the source.
5. Do not cite search snippets as if they were full-paper evidence.
