**Title:** Toward Trustworthy and Transferable SOH/RUL Estimation for Lithium-Ion Batteries: A Critical Review and Multi-Fidelity Validation Framework from Laboratory Cells to Real-World Packs  
**Venue:** Batteries, 12(7), 255

## Scope reported
The review synthesises 176 works and discusses model-based, ML, deep learning, transfer learning, physics-informed, impedance, thermographic and other methods. It frames trustworthiness around observability, robustness, uncertainty calibration, transferability and deployment feasibility.

## Contribution reported
A multi-fidelity validation framework connects synthetic cells, controlled ageing, module/pack testing, fleet shadow operation and safety-governed deployment.

## Relevance
This is an important recent synthesis. BatteryMind AI cannot claim that trustworthy validation, transferability, or cell-to-pack validation has not been discussed. The paper's evidence map may help identify a more specific experimental gap.
